Imprint Method: Full Color P-36

Full Color

Water-based inkjet ink printed directly into the garment fibers, laying down full-color artwork as a soft, flat image with no added surface layer. P-35

Direct-to-garment printing jets CMYK ink (with a white underbase on dark fabric) straight onto a pre-treated garment, then heat-cures it into the fibers. It reproduces photographs, gradients, and dense multi-color artwork with fine detail and a soft hand, since the ink soaks into the cloth rather than sitting on top. Color is a full-color process blend that lands close to brand references rather than as exact spot inks, and prints most vividly on cotton and light fabrics.

Best for: Photographic, gradient, or many-color artwork on cotton and light apparel, where a soft hand and fine detail matter more than exact solid color.
